# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

## POL-CAN-v1#1 Purpose

This policy defines notice requirements and charges when an appointment is
cancelled or a customer does not attend.

## POL-CAN-v1#2 Definitions

### POL-CAN-v1#2.1 Notice interval

Notice is the elapsed time from the recorded cancellation event to the scheduled
appointment start, evaluated in the appointment location's timezone.

### POL-CAN-v1#2.2 Initiating party

The initiating party is the customer, provider, or business that caused the
cancellation. It is not necessarily the employee or system that recorded it.

## POL-CAN-v1#3 Customer cancellations

### POL-CAN-v1#3.1 Standard notice

A customer cancellation with at least 48 hours' notice has no fee.

### POL-CAN-v1#3.2 Late cancellation

A customer cancellation with less than 48 hours' notice may incur a flat $60 late
cancellation fee. The charge cannot exceed the quoted primary-service price.

### POL-CAN-v1#3.3 No-show

A customer who has not checked in within 15 minutes after the scheduled start may
be marked no-show. The fee is the quoted primary-service price, capped at $120.

## POL-CAN-v1#4 Provider or business cancellations

### POL-CAN-v1#4.1 Unfulfilled appointment

No cancellation or no-show fee is owed when the provider or Luma initiates the
cancellation and the booked service is not fulfilled. Any such captured fee must be
returned to the original tender.

### POL-CAN-v1#4.2 Accepted replacement

If the customer accepts a qualified replacement provider at the same location and
the service is completed within 30 minutes of the booked start, the appointment is
treated as fulfilled rather than cancelled.

## POL-CAN-v1#5 Exceptions

### POL-CAN-v1#5.1 Emergency exception

A location lead may waive a customer fee for a documented emergency. The exception
must record a structured reason and approval.

## POL-CAN-v1#6 Records

### POL-CAN-v1#6.1 Insufficient cancellation record

If the initiating party, event time, or appointment identity is missing or
contradictory, staff must investigate before retaining, waiving, or returning a fee.
