# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

| Metadata | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy ID | `POL-CAN` |
| Version | `2` |
| Effective from | 2025-07-01 00:00 local time |
| Effective through | Open-ended |
| Scope | All Luma locations and appointment services |
| Supersedes | `POL-CAN` version 1 |

## POL-CAN-v2#1 Purpose

This policy defines notice requirements and charges when an appointment is
cancelled or a customer does not attend.

## POL-CAN-v2#2 Definitions

### POL-CAN-v2#2.1 Notice interval

Notice is the elapsed time from the cancellation event to the scheduled start,
evaluated using timezone-aware instants and displayed in the appointment location's
local timezone.

### POL-CAN-v2#2.2 Initiating party

The initiating party is the customer, provider, or business that caused the
cancellation. The author who records an event does not determine initiation.

### POL-CAN-v2#2.3 Primary-service price

The primary-service price is the historical quoted price excluding add-ons,
gratuity, tax, and previously assessed fees.

## POL-CAN-v2#3 Customer cancellations and no-shows

### POL-CAN-v2#3.1 Standard notice

A customer cancellation with at least 24 hours' notice has no cancellation fee.

### POL-CAN-v2#3.2 Late cancellation

A customer cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice may incur 50 percent of the
quoted primary-service price, capped at $80.

### POL-CAN-v2#3.3 No-show

A customer who has not checked in within 15 minutes after the scheduled start may
be marked no-show. The fee is the quoted primary-service price, capped at $150.

## POL-CAN-v2#4 Provider or business disruption

### POL-CAN-v2#4.1 Unfulfilled appointment

A customer owes no cancellation or no-show fee when the provider or Luma initiated
the cancellation and no accepted replacement service was fulfilled. An uncaptured
fee is waived; a captured fee is refundable to its original tender.

### POL-CAN-v2#4.2 Accepted provider transfer

When the booked provider becomes unavailable, Luma may offer a qualified replacement
at the same location. If the customer accepts and the same service is completed
within 30 minutes of the booked start, the service obligation remains valid. A
provider reassignment is not itself a cancellation and does not make the completed
service payment refundable.

### POL-CAN-v2#4.3 Rescheduled replacement

If a provider or Luma initiates a reschedule and the customer accepts a different
time, the original appointment must link to the replacement. No cancellation fee is
owed on the original reservation. Payment allocation to the replacement must be
recorded explicitly.

## POL-CAN-v2#5 Exceptions

### POL-CAN-v2#5.1 Emergency exception

An operations employee may waive one late-cancellation or no-show charge for a
documented emergency. The reason and approval are recorded. An unverified exception
request is not by itself proof that the standard fee is wrong.

### POL-CAN-v2#5.2 Location closure

Weather, safety, utility, or building closures initiated by Luma are business
cancellations under section 4.1 even when an automated process recorded the events.

## POL-CAN-v2#6 Records

### POL-CAN-v2#6.1 Insufficient or conflicting evidence

Staff must not make a definitive fee correction when appointment identity,
initiating party, event time, fee association, or applicable version is missing or
contradictory. Corrections append events rather than editing the disputed history.
